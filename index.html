03 / ABOUT ME

Writer by instinct.
Strategist by practice.

For more than 25 years, I've helped organizations find the right words to explain what they do, why it matters, and why people should care.

My work spans brand storytelling, website copy, campaigns, thought leadership, and communications that make complex scientific, technical, and healthcare information accessible. I've been a journalist, copywriter, editor, brand strategist, and marketing leader. Sometimes I've directed the creative process, but I've often been the person doing the writing myself.

I bring the curiosity of a journalist, the discipline of an editor, and the strategic thinking of a marketer. The goal is always the same: writing that's accurate, useful, memorable, and unmistakably human.

FINCH BRANDS / THOUGHT LEADERSHIP

Brand Ho!

May 4, 2012 · Writing by William Stone

We all know that hundreds of millions of years ago, we crawled out of the prehistoric muck to walk on land. Less well known is the fact that at one stage in their evolution, whales did so as well (although, in their case, they probably waddled like sea lions and, like sea lions, almost certainly never wandered too far from the water). Eventually they returned permanently to the ocean and lost their land limbs.

The question is why on earth are we telling you about whale evolution in a branding blog? The answer is simple. Evolution, whether in nature or in branding, doesn’t always follow a straight line, but sometimes reverses course.

Successful brands are those that keep pace with their intended consumers so that they remain relevant. What’s old (undesirable) today can become old school (trendy) tomorrow. In the blink of an eye your “progressive” approach may no longer resonate, while tactics that you thought ran out of steam long ago suddenly receive new surges of life. That’s why you should constantly test and re-test your assumptions.

Take BlackBerry for example. With the increasing popularity of consumer-focused Android and iOS devices, the brand shifted their focus away from corporate customers and attempted to compete in the consumer-oriented market. In late March, Research in Motion (RIM), the maker of BlackBerry, announced that the brand would return to its roots, focusing solely on products that can be used productively in and out of the workplace to help boost workflow. “We can’t do everything ourselves, but we can do what we’re good at,” Thorsten Hein, CEO of RIM said in explanation.

Business and nature are both unpredictable, but survival in either depends on how swiftly one can adapt…and adapt back if research shows it’s necessary.
